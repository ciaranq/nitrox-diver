# Module 5: Emergencies, Agency Variations & Exam Cheat Sheet

## 1. Handling Nitrox Emergencies

### Suspected CNS Oxygen Toxicity Underwater
- **Warning signs (twitching, tunnel vision, tinnitus, dizziness, nausea):**
  1. Signal buddy immediately and **begin a controlled ascent** (reducing depth immediately lowers $PpO_2$!).
  2. Switch to a lower-$O_2$ gas if carrying one (rare in single-tank rec diving — ascending is the fastest fix).
  3. Abort the dive, complete a safe ascent/safety stop if conscious and safe.
- **Full underwater convulsion:**
  1. **Hold the regulator in the diver's mouth** if it's still in — **NEVER force a regulator back into a clenched jaw** during the tonic (stiff) phase.
  2. Wait through the convulsion (~1–2 minutes) while holding the diver neutrally buoyant; once the clonic/relaxation phase starts and breathing resumes, ascend safely protecting the airway (avoid holding their breath during rapid ascent -> arterial gas embolism risk).
  3. Surface, establish positive buoyancy, drop weights, call emergency services, and administer **100% emergency oxygen** on the boat/shore.

### Suspected Decompression Sickness (DCS)
- Even on Nitrox, if DCS is suspected: give **100% surface oxygen** via non-rebreather/demand valve, keep diver horizontal/warm, hydrate if conscious, and contact **DAN / emergency medical evacuation** (note: in Australia, DAN Asia-Pacific / `000`).
- **Why give 100% $O_2$ to a Nitrox diver with DCS?** Breathing 100% $O_2$ has $0\%$ $N_2$, creating the maximum nitrogen pressure gradient to off-gas nitrogen from bubbles and oxygenate hypoxic tissues.

### RAID Emergency, Pre-Dive & Lifestyle Rules (Often Tested!)
- **Pre-dive buddy check (`BRAID`):** **B**uoyancy & Weights, **R**egulators & Releases, **A**ir/Gas checks, **I**nstruments, **D**iver OK to dive.
- **Computer failure underwater:** Ascend at $9\text{ m/min}$, stop at **$10\text{ m}$ for $2\text{ min}$** (if deeper than $10\text{ m}$), stop at **$5\text{ m}$ for $10\text{ min}$** (or $3–6\text{ m}$ as long as gas safely allows), and stay out of the water **24 hours**.
- **Flying / Freediving / Exceeding $1.6\text{ bar}$:**
  - Wait **12–24 hours** before flying (follow computer No-Fly).
  - Wait **24 hours** after scuba before freediving.
  - If you exceed **$PpO_2 = 1.6\text{ bar}$** underwater, treat as **100% CNS exposure** and do not dive for **24 hours**.
- **Health factors tested in RAID Academic:** Smoking paralyzes lung cilia (~20 min) & constricts vessels; alcohol dehydrates & worsens narcosis; **trans-fatty meals** cause blood "slugging" peaking ~6 hours post-meal, raising DCS risk.

## 3. One-Page Exam Formula Cheat Sheet

Memorize these 4 formulas cold before sitting any exam:

1. **Absolute Pressure (bar):**
   $$P_{\text{abs}} = \frac{D}{10} + 1$$
2. **Partial Pressure of Oxygen ($PpO_2$):**
   $$PpO_2 = FO_2 \times P_{\text{abs}}$$
3. **Maximum Operating Depth (MOD) — *ROUND DOWN*:**
   $$\text{MOD} = \left(\frac{PpO_{2,\max}}{FO_2} - 1\right) \times 10$$
4. **Best Mix ($FO_2$) — *ROUND DOWN*:**
   $$\text{Best } FO_2 = \frac{PpO_{2,\max}}{P_{\text{abs}}}$$
5. **Equivalent Air Depth (EAD) — *ROUND UP*:**
   $$\text{EAD} = \left(\frac{1 - FO_2}{0.79} \times P_{\text{abs}} - 1\right) \times 10$$

### Quick Sanity Checks on Exam Day
- **MOD check:** EAN32 @ $1.4\text{ bar}$ = **$33\text{ m}$** ($33.75$); EAN36 @ $1.4\text{ bar}$ = **$28\text{ m}$** ($28.88$); EAN40 @ $1.4\text{ bar}$ = **$25\text{ m}$**.
- **Rounding direction:** MOD = **DOWN**, Best Mix = **DOWN**, EAD = **UP**.
- **Analyzed mix:** You **personally** analyze it, confirm within $\pm 1\%$, and sign/label **$FO_2\%$ + MOD + Date + Signature**.
