# Module 2: Oxygen Toxicity & Maximum Operating Depth (MOD)

## 1. Two Types of Oxygen Toxicity

| Type | Also Called | Cause | Recreational Risk? | Key Symptoms |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **CNS Toxicity** | Acute / Paul Bert effect | High $PpO_2$ (**$> 1.4\text{ bar}$**, sharply rising $> 1.6\text{ bar}$) | **Primary hazard** in Nitrox diving | **CONVENTID** (see below) — **convulsion underwater = likely drowning** |
| **Pulmonary Toxicity** | Whole-body / Lorrain Smith effect | Prolonged exposure to lower $PpO_2$ ($> 0.5\text{ bar}$ for many hours) | Very rare in single recreational dives; tracked via **OTU** | Dry cough, chest tightness, burning throat, reduced lung capacity |

### CNS Symptoms Mnemonic: **CONVENTID**
- **Con**vulsions (often **with no warning signs** — the #1 danger!)
- **V**isual disturbances (tunnel vision, blurred vision)
- **E**ars / ringing (tinnitus)
- **N**ausea
- **T**witching (especially lips, face, fingers)
- **I**rritability / mood changes / anxiety
- **D**izziness

> **Exam Rule:** CNS convulsions can occur **without any prior warning symptoms**. Never exceed your planned $PpO_2$ limit.

## 2. Standard Recreational $PpO_2$ Limits & TOD vs MOD

- **$1.4\text{ bar}$ ($1.4\text{ ATA}$):** **Maximum working limit** for all recreational Nitrox dives (PADI, RAID, SSI, SDI, BSAC).
- **$1.6\text{ bar}$ ($1.6\text{ ATA}$):** **Absolute ceiling / contingency limit** (never planned as working depth in recreational diving; used only for unexpected depth excursion calculations or decompression stops in tech/BSAC Advanced).

> **CRITICAL AGENCY TERMINOLOGY TRAP (RAID vs PADI/SSI):**
> - **PADI / SSI / BSAC** call the $1.4\text{ bar}$ depth your **MOD** (working) and $1.6\text{ bar}$ your contingency MOD.
> - **RAID's Academic Manual** distinguishes two terms:
>   - **TOD (Target Operating Depth)** = depth at **$PpO_2 = 1.4\text{ bar}$** (your planned max depth).
>   - **MOD (Maximum Operating Depth)** = depth at **$PpO_2 = 1.6\text{ bar}$** (hard ceiling you must never cross).
> - **RAID Rule if $1.6\text{ bar}$ is exceeded:** Treat as **100% CNS exposure** and **do not dive for 24 hours**.

## 3. Maximum Operating Depth (MOD)

**MOD** is the deepest depth you can go without exceeding your chosen $PpO_2$ limit.

$$\text{MOD (m)} = \left(\frac{PpO_{2,\max}}{FO_2} - 1\right) \times 10$$

> **CRITICAL EXAM RULE: Always round MOD DOWN (more conservative), never up.**

## 4. CNS Oxygen Clock (NOAA Limits)

Your dive computer tracks accumulated CNS% using $PpO_2$ and time. Never exceed **100% CNS** in a single dive or 24-hour period.

| $PpO_2$ (bar) | Max Single Dive Time | Max Daily (24 hr) |
|---|---|---|
| **$1.6$** *(contingency)* | $45\text{ min}$ | $150\text{ min}$ |
| **$1.5$** | $120\text{ min}$ | $180\text{ min}$ |
| **$1.4$** *(rec max)* | $150\text{ min}$ | $180\text{ min}$ |
| **$1.3$** | $180\text{ min}$ | $210\text{ min}$ |
| **$1.2$** | $210\text{ min}$ | $240\text{ min}$ |
| **$1.1$** | $240\text{ min}$ | $270\text{ min}$ |
| **$1.0$** | $300\text{ min}$ | $300\text{ min}$ |

- **Surface interval CNS recovery:** CNS percentage drops with a **90-minute half-time** at the surface ($1\text{ bar}$ air) — after 90 min on the surface, remaining CNS% is halved. *(RAID's CNS Surface Interval Credit Table rounds $PpO_2$ UP to the next table row and end-of-dive CNS% UP to the next 10% tier; `1:31–2:00` hrs cuts CNS% by 50%).*
- **OTU (Oxygen Tolerance Units):** Tracked for multi-day diving. RAID Table 1B caps daily OTU at **850 OTU** (1 day), **700/day** (2 days), **620/day** (3 days), scaling down to **300 OTU/day** for 11+ day trips. *(Example: 20 min @ $1.4\text{ bar}$ = $20 \times 1.63\text{ OTU/min} = 32.6\text{ OTU}$.)*
