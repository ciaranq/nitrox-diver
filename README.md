## Recommended Workflow to Get Certified Cheaply

1. **Read** [cheapest-path-sydney.md](cheapest-path-sydney.md) and call/book the cheapest dry course (currently **Pro Dive Sydney St Peters PADI Dry from $199 AUD**, or bundle with AOW if already planning AOW, or check a live RAID centre/instructor via `diveraid.com`).
2. **Optional free pre-study:** Create a free account at `diveraid.com` to access RAID's free eLearning manual alongside this repo's syllabus.
3. **Study & Drill:** Work through `syllabus/01`–`05` and ask your AI tutor in chat to quiz you module-by-module until you score **90%+** on [quizzes/mock-exam.md](quizzes/mock-exam.md).
4. **Certify:** Attend your ~1.5-hour dive-shop gas-analysis workshop, analyze & label a cylinder accurately, pass the agency exam, and receive your digital Nitrox card.
