## Part A: Concept Questions (20 Questions)

1. What is the maximum oxygen percentage covered by standard recreational Enriched Air Nitrox?

   - D) Charles' Law

   - A) $1.4\text{ bar}$

   - C) $1.6\text{ bar}$
   - D) $1.8\text{ bar}$

7. What is the **single most dangerous consequence** of CNS oxygen toxicity underwater?
   - A) Persistent dry cough after the dive
   - B) Sudden convulsion underwater leading to regulator loss and drowning

8. Which statement about CNS oxygen toxicity warning signs (CONVENTID) is true?

### Part B Worked Answers
21. $P_{\text{abs}} = (25/10) + 1 = 3.5\text{ bar}$. $PpO_2 = 0.36 \times 3.5 = \mathbf{1.26\text{ bar}}$.
22. $\text{MOD} = ((1.4 / 0.32) - 1) \times 10 = (4.375 - 1) \times 10 = 33.75\text{ m} \rightarrow \mathbf{33\text{ m}}$ (or $33.7\text{ m}$, rounded DOWN).
23. $\text{MOD} = ((1.4 / 0.38) - 1) \times 10 = (3.684 - 1) \times 10 = 26.84\text{ m} \rightarrow \mathbf{26\text{ m}}$ (rounded DOWN).
24. $P_{\text{abs}} = (28/10) + 1 = 3.8\text{ bar}$. $\text{Best } FO_2 = 1.4 / 3.8 = 0.3684 = 36.84\% \rightarrow \mathbf{36\%\ (\text{EAN36})}$ (rounded DOWN).
25. $P_{\text{abs}} = (20/10) + 1 = 3.0\text{ bar}$. $FN_2 = 0.64$. $\text{EAD} = ((0.64 / 0.79) \times 3.0 - 1) \times 10 = (0.8101 \times 3.0 - 1) \times 10 = (2.430 - 1) \times 10 = 14.3\text{ m} \rightarrow \mathbf{15\text{ m}}$ (rounded UP).

### Part C RAID-Specific Answers
26. **TOD (Target Operating Depth)** is the planned depth limit at **$1.4\text{ bar}$** $PpO_2$; **MOD (Maximum Operating Depth)** in RAID's Academic manual is the absolute ceiling at **$1.6\text{ bar}$** $PpO_2$ you must never cross.
27. Enter **`32%`** — never round UP fractional percentages when programming a dive computer, as rounding up understates nitrogen loading.
28. Treat the dive as **100% CNS exposure** and remain **out of the water for 24 hours**.
29. Do **not** use a **grease pencil** (it smudges); use a **permanent marker**.
30. **$20\text{ m}$** — Nitrox certification never overrides your base diving depth qualification.
