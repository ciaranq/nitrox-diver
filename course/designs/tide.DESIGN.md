# Design: Tide (default)

**Mood:** glassmorphic, vibrant, underwater. Frosted panels over deep teal gradients; the yellow cylinder with green nitrox band is the signature motif.

## Type
Display Bricolage Grotesque, body Inter, mono JetBrains Mono.

## Shape
Radius 14px. Soft, long, low-opacity drop shadows. Translucent surfaces.

## Do
- Keep accents teal; use yellow and green only for tank and pass states.
- Use translucency and radial background glow.

## Don't
- No hard borders or flat shadows (that is Logbook).
- No serif faces.
